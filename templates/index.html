Check a message before you act on it

Paste the text of a suspicious email or SMS, and the link it points to if it has one. PhishLens checks both against a set of known phishing patterns and explains exactly what it found.

Educational tool only. A "low" result is not a guarantee of safety — when in doubt, verify through an official channel you already trust rather than any contact details in the message itself.
